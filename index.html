FAQ

Questions Before You Start

Do you offer in-person or virtual training?
Both. Personal training is available in person or virtually over video — same programming, same coaching, same accountability. Small group training is in person only.
Who is personal training for?
Anyone who wants real coaching — beginners learning the basics, people coming back after time off, and lifters dealing with nagging pain or plateaus. If you've got an injury history or movement limitations, the corrective exercise focus is built exactly for that.
What does nutrition coaching include?
Personalized macro targets, regular check-ins, and lifestyle coaching around habits like sleep, stress, and meal planning. It's flexible coaching built around foods you actually eat — not a rigid meal plan.
Are workout plans included with nutrition coaching?
No — nutrition coaching is nutrition and lifestyle only. If you want training too, personal training sessions include nutrition coaching, or you can pair nutrition coaching with one of the digital workout plans from the shop.
How does small group training work?
Groups are capped at 4 people so everyone gets real coaching. You can sign up with friends or family, or get matched into a group at a similar level. Sessions are in person and cost $35 per person per session.
How do I get started?
Fill out the coaching application — it takes a few minutes and asks about your goals, current fitness level, which service you're interested in, and any injuries or limitations. Abby reviews every application personally and will reach out about next steps.
